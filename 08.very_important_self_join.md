# Solutiom

```sql
-- Select unique members who have recommended at least one other member
SELECT DISTINCT
    a.firstname,
    a.surname
FROM cd.members a

-- Self join: match a member to another member they recommended
JOIN cd.members b
    ON a.memid = b.recommendedby

-- Sort the results alphabetically by surname, then firstname
ORDER BY
    a.surname,
    a.firstname;
```
